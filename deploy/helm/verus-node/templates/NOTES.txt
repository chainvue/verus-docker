{{ include "verus-node.fullname" . }} is installing chain {{ .Values.chain }}.

Watch it come up:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }} -w

The pod becomes READY only once the chain is fully synced. That is intentional:
readiness means "safe to serve RPC". Until then it is RUNNING but not READY,
which is the normal state during initial sync and can last hours or days.

Follow progress:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec -it {{ include "verus-node.fullname" . }}-0 -c verus -- healthcheck.sh

Run a CLI command:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec -it {{ include "verus-node.fullname" . }}-0 -c verus -- verus getinfo
